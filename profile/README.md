## 🌟 Our Mission

At **GiveBack2People**, we believe that top-tier design tools, educational materials, and creative assets should be accessible to everyone—regardless of budget, background, or geographic boundary. 

Our mission is simple:
* 🎯 **Curate the Best:** Filter out the noise, paywalls, and low-quality spam to surface genuinely useful, free, and accessible resources.
* 🤝 **Give Back to the Community:** Build open-source collections, interactive guides, and accessible platforms that foster learning and creativity.
* 🛡️ **Ethical & Clean:** Champion legal, royalty-free, and ethically sourced tools and assets.

## 🤝 Get Involved

GiveBack2People is a community effort, and we welcome contributions from all skill levels!

* 💡 **Submit a Resource:** Found an awesome free tool or open-source library? [Open a resource submission](https://github.com/GiveBack2People/designresources/issues/new/choose).
* 🐛 **Report an Issue:** Found a broken link or outdated description? [Let us know](https://github.com/GiveBack2People/designresources/issues/new/choose).
* ✍️ **Improve Descriptions:** Help us refine and enrich catalog descriptions for new learners.

Read our [Contributing Guidelines](https://github.com/GiveBack2People/designresources/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/GiveBack2People/designresources/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) to get started.
